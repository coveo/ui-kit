The library is now the Converse SDK, published as `@coveo/converse`. It was published as `@coveo/thermidor`, which gets no further releases. To upgrade, replace `@coveo/thermidor` with `@coveo/converse` in your dependencies and imports, including `@coveo/thermidor/private`, which becomes `@coveo/converse/private`.
