The internal `QuanticRadioButtonsGroup` component is deleted. Quantic no longer uses it. If one of your components uses `c-quantic-radio-buttons-group`, replace it with a `lightning-radio-group` with `type="button"` for text options, or with a `lightning-button-group` of `lightning-button-icon-stateful` for icon options.
