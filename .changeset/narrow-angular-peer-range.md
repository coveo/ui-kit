'@coveo/atomic-angular': patch

Narrow the supported Angular range from `14 - 21` to `^18 || ^19 || ^20 || ^21`.

Angular 14 and 15 were never installable: `@coveo/atomic` and `@coveo/headless` both require `typescript >=5.0.0`, while Angular 14 caps TypeScript at `<4.9` and Angular 15 at `<5.0`. Those ranges are disjoint, so no consumer on those majors could have a coherent install.

Angular 16 and 17 install, but cannot type-check against this package as generated by their own CLI. Both emit `moduleResolution: node`, which predates the `exports` field. The wrapper's types reach `@coveo/atomic/components` and `@coveo/headless/commerce`, which are reachable only through `exports` subpaths, so neither resolves. On Angular 16 the build fails outright with `TS2307`. On Angular 17 the same errors are suppressed by the CLI's `skipLibCheck: true`, and the wrapper's type surface silently collapses to nothing, so binding any input such as `[field]` raises `NG8002` against a component the compiler believes has no inputs.

Angular 18 is the first major whose CLI emits `moduleResolution: bundler`, and it type-checks cleanly. 20 and 21 emit `module: preserve`, which implies the same resolution.
